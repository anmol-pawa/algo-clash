# Vowel Census

A lighthouse keeper counts the vowels in every message that arrives by signal flag. The vowels are `a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`, and a vowel counts in both upper and lower case. The letter `y` is not a vowel here.

Given the message `s`, return how many of its characters are vowels.

## Examples

- `s = "Harbor"` gives `2` (`a` and `o`).
- `s = "AEIOU sky"` gives `5`.
- `s = "rhythm"` gives `0`.

## Constraints

- `0 <= s.length() <= 100000`
- `s` contains only English letters and spaces.
